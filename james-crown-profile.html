What I do, in plain terms

Tap an area to see what it means and an example.

Business transformation

Reshaping how teams, roles and processes fit together so the organisation runs the way it needs to.

Example: Redesigned the operating model to move a global logistics firm's finance teams into shared services.

Process optimisation & design

Mapping how work happens today, designing a better version, and getting it signed off.

Example: Leading process optimisation and new process design for a multi-brand tech group, from discovery through to senior sign-off.

AI consultancy & automation

Spotting where AI and automation remove manual effort, then designing processes around them.

Example: Designed processes for AI-driven SEO audits across c.12 brands, automating issue detection, ticketing and reconciliation.

Continuous improvement

Using Lean methods to remove waste step by step, and leaving teams with habits that keep improving.

Example: £1.65m in annual savings across food FMCG sites through Lean methodologies.

Operational excellence & KPIs

Building the measures, dashboards and models that show what's working and where the money goes.

Example: Transport modelling for a fuel distributor that set a strategy to cut transport costs by 17%.

Change & project leadership

Running workstreams, plans and risks, and bringing people along from frontline teams to the C-suite.

Example: Led operational readiness for a fast-tracked software rollout, using gap analysis to shape future processes and roles.
